# CYPURRPUNK patch notes (for Discord)

Each update below is ready to paste into Discord: open this file, copy everything INSIDE the grey
box (between the ``` lines), paste it into your channel. Discord shows the `#` headings, **bold**
and `-` bullets properly. Each post is kept under Discord's 2000-character limit.

Claude adds a line to "NEXT UPDATE" every time something players would notice is finished.
When you want to post, ask Claude for "the patch notes": it tidies the draft into a dated post,
gives it to you, and starts a fresh draft.

## NEXT UPDATE (draft, not posted yet)

```text
## 🐾 New
- **NEON BAT:** the first melee weapon! $20 at both shops. Hold to keep swinging: it smacks everything right in front of you and knocks it back. Works on VAC-COPs and the rival gang (never your own). One paw, so you can hold two.
- **Starting money:** new cats start with $100, so you can gear up before your first trip. Cats you already have with less get topped up to $100 once.
- **Quick use slots:** catnip and catdip now go into two quick slots (keys **1** / **2**, d-pad left / right on a controller), up to 3 of each. One press and you're zooming, no more swapping them into your paws.
- **Jedi flip:** a catdip double jump is now a front flip.
- **SCRAP:** MEGA VAC-COPs now drop glowing scrap. It's worth no cash, stays in your bag when you extract, and can be saved in your stash.
- **THE MAINFRAMES:** the giant computer in each gang's sewer is alive! Each sealed district drains 25%, and each scrap you feed it (E) adds 25%.
- **Your exit, your power:** your gang's Mainframe decides when your exit opens: 60 heat at 100%, 75 at 75%, 82 at 50%, 90 at 25%, and a last-second 95 at 0%.
- **Hack the rivals:** at 100%, scrap drains the rival gang's Mainframe by 25% instead, and everyone sees it in the feed.
- **Keep it charged:** the lowest gang's Mainframe sets the city's start. At 50% and below, districts start with heat already up, and at 0% a MEGA VAC-COP is waiting up top. If every gang is at 100% when you head up, OVERCLOCK makes heat rise 20% slower.
- **Status bar:** the top-right bar shows your gang's Mainframe in the sewers, and an EXIT mark on the heat bar shows when your exit opens.

## 🔧 Changes
- **Solo is back to the classic layout:** you come up in the centre of the city with one car parked right there, and there's one exit.
- **Golden Koi:** MEGA VAC-COPs no longer drop it. There's just the one hidden in the city now.
- **Inventory at the stash:** your loot bag now stays visible above your stash.

## 🐛 Fixes
- Cars could get stuck in the middle of town at the start of a game instead of waiting at your gang's corner.
```

## Posted updates

### 2026-10-29

```text
# 😼 CYPURRPUNK — Update 29 Oct 2026
**TWO GANGS. ONE CITY.**

## 🐾 New
- **Pick a gang:** the **Catleone Family** (Don Catleone) or the **Burrow Gang** (Pawnie & Clawd). Each has its own sewer hideout, shop, boss and car.
- **PvP:** your bolts now hurt the rival gang. Friendly fire is off.
- **Downed, not dead:** at 0 HP you go down and crawl. A gang-mate holds E for 3 s to pick you back up. Nobody comes in 25 s = knocked out.
- **Rob them:** hold E on a downed rival for 2 s to steal their whole loot bag.
- **Each gang gets its own way in and out:** manholes in opposite corners of the city, and an exit that glows in your gang's colour. The payphone only shows the exit to your gang.
- **One trip per district:** extract or get knocked out and you sit tight until the next district.
- **Kill feed** (top left): downs, knockouts, revives and robberies, in gang colours.
- **Couch co-op:** on the lobby screen, player 1 can press START on their controller to hand it to a new player.

## 🔧 Changes
- You pop out of the manhole facing up the street, with your gang's car right in front of you.
- Couch players now join in the lobby only (no dropping in mid-game). Controller died? START on a spare one takes over that player.
- The revive meter fills the downed cat's health bar. Fewer popups.
- Golden koi no longer adds heat when you grab it.
- Once everyone is home and has used their trip, the heat races to 100 so nobody waits forever.

## 🐛 Fixes
- The title screen no longer sets off alarms and a DEEP CLEAN behind the menu.
- Online joiners were sometimes refused their trip up top.
- Payphones kept ringing for gangs with nobody left up top.
- Downed cats waited for gang-mates who couldn't come.
- Cats coming home at the same moment got launched into the air.
- Mid-game couch joiners always ended up in the Catleone Family.

Stress-tested with 8 players on 4 PCs.
```
