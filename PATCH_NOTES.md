# CYPURRPUNK patch notes (for Discord)

Each update below is ready to paste into Discord: open this file, copy everything INSIDE the grey
box (between the ``` lines), paste it into your channel. Discord shows the `#` headings, **bold**
and `-` bullets properly. Each post is kept under Discord's 2000-character limit.

Claude adds a line to "NEXT UPDATE" every time something players would notice is finished.
When you want to post, ask Claude for "the patch notes": it tidies the draft into a dated post,
gives it to you, and starts a fresh draft.

## NEXT UPDATE (draft, not posted yet)

```text
## 🐾 New
- **One-click downloads:** grab the newest build for Windows, Linux or Android from the front page at github.com/natjster/CYPURRPUNK. The links update by themselves every time the game does.
- **Invite Codes:** play with friends in other houses, usually with no router setup! Host a game and it opens the door by itself, then gives you a code like MEOW-499A-21B0. Copy it from the pause menu and send it over. Your friend hits PASTE CODE, then JOIN. If your router says no, the game tells you, and the guide has easy fixes.
- **THE SEWER BREACH:** let your Mainframe hit 0% and OVERCLEAN comes for your home. You get one last raid at 0% to bring home scrap, then a 15-second siren to feed it in. Fail, and the blast gate on the new north tunnel slides up and 35 bots plus 2 MEGA VAC-COPs pour in. Your sewer is no safe zone any more: guns work, nobody heals. Last 30 seconds with one cat on its feet for a miracle: every bot explodes and your Mainframe reboots to 25%. Get wiped out and the rival gang wins!
- **The sewer fights back:** when the bots break in, everyone at home grabs a gun! The Don sprays his tommy gun, Nonna lobs bouncing cucumbers, Frankie waves a red laser around the room, and all of them yell battle cries. They couldn't hit a barn, but they'll chip away at the front line. Get a bot too close and they're flat on their back until it's over.
- **Live feed:** while a rival gang's sewer is being breached, your screen switches to their security camera so you can watch it all go wrong.
- **SQUIRT-DRONES:** OVERCLEAN's new quadcopters hover above the street, too high for your car. Get caught in their red spotlight and the heat climbs, and they fire water zaps at wherever you're standing, so keep moving! A Yarn-Zooka stun drops them low enough to whack.
- **LINT-TRAPPERS:** sticky mop-bots that leave glowing pink glue behind them and lob it at your feet. Step in it and you're at half speed for 3 seconds, even with zoomies, so the VAC-COPs can catch you. Jump over the puddles to stay clean!
- **5 ridiculous new weapons at both shops:**
  - **FROZEN TUNA ($80, 2 paws):** a giant frozen fish. Slow, wide swings that launch cats and cars sky-high.
  - **THE RED DOT ($90, 1 paw):** a tiny laser pointer. Hold it on a target and watch it melt, until it overheats.
  - **HAIRBALL BLASTER ($120, 1 paw):** a short-range scattergun that fires a wall of neon hairballs. Hold two!
  - **YARN-ZOOKA ($160, 2 paws):** an electric blast that stuns every VAC-COP nearby for 6 seconds, even bosses.
  - **CUCUMBER LAUNCHER ($280, 2 paws):** a huge green explosion that destroys VAC-COPs and throws cats and cars across the street.
- **NEON BAT:** the first melee weapon! $20 at both shops. Hold to keep swinging: it smacks everything right in front of you and knocks it back. Works on VAC-COPs and the rival gang (never your own). One paw, so you can hold two.
- **Starting money:** new cats start with $100, so you can gear up before your first trip. Cats you already have with less get topped up to $100 once.
- **Quick use slots:** catnip and catdip now go into two quick slots (keys **1** / **2**, d-pad left / right on a controller), up to 3 of each. One press and you're zooming, no more swapping them into your paws.
- **Jedi flip:** a catdip double jump is now a front flip.
- **SCRAP:** MEGA VAC-COPs now drop glowing scrap. It's worth no cash, stays in your bag when you extract, and can be saved in your stash.
- **THE MAINFRAMES:** the giant computer in each gang's sewer is alive! Each sealed district drains 25%, and each scrap you feed it (E) adds 25%.
- **Your exit, your power:** your gang's Mainframe decides when your exit opens: 60 heat at 100%, 75 at 75%, 82 at 50%, 90 at 25%, and a last-second 95 at 0%.
- **Hack the rivals:** at 100%, scrap drains the rival gang's Mainframe by 25% instead, and everyone sees it in the feed.
- **Keep it charged:** the lowest gang's Mainframe sets the city's start. At 50% and below, districts start with heat already up, and at 0% a MEGA VAC-COP is waiting up top. If every gang is at 100% when you head up, OVERCLOCK makes heat rise 20% slower.
- **Status bar:** the top-right bar shows your gang's Mainframe in the sewers, and an EXIT mark on the heat bar shows when your exit opens.
- **The sewers are full of characters:** 9 new cats to meet, for 5 quest givers in each gang's sewer. The Catleones get Paulie "No-Brooms", Nonna Catleone, Frankie "Espresso" and Sal "The Slapper". The Burrow gets Billy The Kitten, Bumper-Car Benny, "Gold-Tooth" Gus, Jittery Jax and Calamity Clara.
- **4 new repeatable quests:** smash 5 VAC-COPs ($150), extract with a Golden Koi ($250 on top of its value), extract at 85+ heat ($150), and disrespect the opposition by destroying a MEGA VAC-COP, robbing a downed rival or slapping one with a melee weapon ($200). Get knocked out and your kill and disrespect progress goes back to 0.
- **The Night of the Big Red Button:** ask around with WHO LET OVERCLEAN IN? and find out what really happened. Everyone has their own version.

## 🔧 Changes
- The old ladder in the sewers is gone: a barred tunnel stands in its place.
- **Each gang has its own sewer now:** the Burrow Gang lives in a rust-and-amber dug-out with timber beams, the Catleones keep their green tunnel.
- **Yarn-Zooka and Cucumber Launcher shots fly:** they arc through the air and go off where they land, and cucumbers bounce once first. Lead your target! Swap guns mid-flight and the shot is lost.
- **The 1% Miracle** now sets the bots off one by one in a chain of explosions instead of all at once.
- **Solo is back to the classic layout:** you come up in the centre of the city with one car parked right there, and there's one exit.
- **Golden Koi:** MEGA VAC-COPs no longer drop it. There's just the one hidden in the city now.
- **Inventory at the stash:** your loot bag now stays visible above your stash.
- **PS1 UI option:** want the full retro look? Settings > Effects > UI Style > PS1 gives the HUD and menus chunky PS1 pixels, and the CRT TV effect bends and scanlines the whole screen together, like a real console on an old TV. Modern (sharp HUD and menus) stays the default.
- **Item labels:** everything on the street now has a sharp name floating over it when you're close, including fish, gold koi and scrap.
- **Picking things up:** no more walking over loot to grab it! Walk up to any item and press the button on the "PICKUP" prompt (it shows your own keyboard key or controller button), just like getting in the car.

## 🐛 Fixes
- **NPCs talk again:** in downloaded builds, the Don and everyone else in the sewers only said "...". All their lines are back.
- Cars could get stuck in the middle of town at the start of a game instead of waiting at your gang's corner.
- A Mainframe hitting 0% started the breach instantly, with no time to feed it and no 0% district to hunt for scrap.
- Items dropped on a sewer floor, including a MEGA VAC-COP's scrap, vanished when a new district started.
- Breach bots no longer vacuum loot out of your bag.
- A grazed bot no longer counts as your kill for quests when the Miracle blows it up.
- Fights in a breached sewer no longer raise the city's heat.
```

```text
# 😼 CYPURRPUNK — Update 29 Oct 2026
**TWO GANGS. ONE CITY.**

## 🐾 New
- **Pick a gang:** the **Catleone Family** (Don Catleone) or the **Burrow Gang** (Pawnie & Clawd). Each has its own sewer hideout, shop, boss and car.
- **PvP:** your bolts now hurt the rival gang. Friendly fire is off.
- **Downed, not dead:** at 0 HP you go down and crawl. A gang-mate holds E for 3 s to pick you back up. Nobody comes in 25 s = knocked out.
- **Rob them:** hold E on a downed rival for 2 s to steal their whole loot bag.
- **Each gang gets its own way in and out:** manholes in opposite corners of the city, and an exit that glows in your gang's colour. The payphone only shows the exit to your gang.
- **One trip per district:** extract or get knocked out and you sit tight until the next district.
- **Kill feed** (top left): downs, knockouts, revives and robberies, in gang colours.
- **Couch co-op:** on the lobby screen, player 1 can press START on their controller to hand it to a new player.

## 🔧 Changes
- You pop out of the manhole facing up the street, with your gang's car right in front of you.
- Couch players now join in the lobby only (no dropping in mid-game). Controller died? START on a spare one takes over that player.
- The revive meter fills the downed cat's health bar. Fewer popups.
- Golden koi no longer adds heat when you grab it.
- Once everyone is home and has used their trip, the heat races to 100 so nobody waits forever.

## 🐛 Fixes
- The title screen no longer sets off alarms and a DEEP CLEAN behind the menu.
- Online joiners were sometimes refused their trip up top.
- Payphones kept ringing for gangs with nobody left up top.
- Downed cats waited for gang-mates who couldn't come.
- Cats coming home at the same moment got launched into the air.
- Mid-game couch joiners always ended up in the Catleone Family.

Stress-tested with 8 players on 4 PCs.
```
